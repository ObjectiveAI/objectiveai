---
title: 'volumes::watch'
summary: "Watch one volume's filesystem: a snapshot, then a frame per change, until the watcher disconnects."
order: 6
---

The answer is a filesystem tree stream — one snapshot, then one frame
per change — the same shape a laboratory's filesystem view uses, over
a different tree. The watch ends when the caller says so or leaves;
there is nothing to unsubscribe beyond that.
