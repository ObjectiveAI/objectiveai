---
title: 'images::check'
summary: 'Ask whether a provider would supply an image to this caller — where no is an answer, not an error.'
order: 10
---

A name and a digest go up; what comes back is the provider's answer
about availability, distinct from the provider failing to answer. The
distinction is the endpoint's whole reason to exist: a deploy would
turn no into an error, and this asks the question without creating
anything.
