---
title: 'Authorization'
summary: "The connection's first frame is a credential, presented by whichever side dialled and never answered."
order: 4
---

Whichever side dialled authenticates: its first frame — nothing may
precede it — carries a credential, in one mode today (*unbrokered*: an
opaque string the two ends agreed on beforehand) with a tag reserved
for a brokered mode to come. There is no answer frame. An accepted
credential is followed by the connection simply working, a rejected
one by a close, and a peer that has not authenticated is owed no bytes
at all — not even an error. On the provider side, the accepted
credential's judgment produces the caller's identity, which every
subsequent operation receives.
