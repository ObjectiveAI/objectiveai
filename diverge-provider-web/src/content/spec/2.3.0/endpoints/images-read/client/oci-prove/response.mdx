```text
[proof: 32 bytes]      exactly once: the proof
```

- **The sequence.** Exactly one channel response, followed by the
  channel response finish, or a channel response finish that no
  channel response precedes.
- **The frame.** Exactly 32 bytes, the proof that
  [Possession](/shared/possession/#the-proof) defines over the image
  of the scope, with the platform and the nonce of the ask, with no tag
  and no header, as [Possession](/shared/possession/#the-answer)
  defines.
- **No proof.** A channel response finish that no channel response
  precedes states that the server cannot supply the bytes of the image
  of the scope, or that the ask breaks the requirement on `platform`
  that [Possession](/shared/possession/#the-image-bytes) states. It
  states no reason.
