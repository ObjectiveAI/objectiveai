```text
[bytes …]      zero or more, the blob
```

- **The sequence.** Zero or more channel responses, each a piece of
  the blob verbatim with no tag and no header, in order; the pieces
  concatenated are the blob. The channel response finish follows the
  last. A channel response finish that no channel response precedes
  states that the server does not hold the digest for the client.
- **The pieces.** The server sends no piece larger than 2 MiB. A
  client appends each piece to what arrived before and measures
  nothing; the finish says the blob is whole, and a channel that
  closes without its finish states that the blob did not all arrive.
