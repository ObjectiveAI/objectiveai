```text
[message …]    zero or more, one message of the relayed connection each
```

- **The sequence.** Zero or more channel responses, each one message of the relayed connection with no tag in front, in the order the
  connector sent them on its
  [frames](/endpoints/daemons-connect/server/frames/) channel. The
  channel response finish is the connector hanging up: it follows
  when the connector finishes its frames channel, when the connect
  scope ends, or when the connector's connection ends. A channel
  response finish that no channel response precedes states that the
  id names no connection whose half may be opened, or that the
  connector hung up before sending a frame.
- **The message.** Each channel response is exactly one message of
  the relayed connection, verbatim as the connector sent it, with no
  tag in front. The first is the connector's authorization frame, the
  offer, and the second is the connector's authorization frame, the
  proof, as [Authorization](/authorization/) states. Every later one
  is one frame of the connector's, sealed under the relayed
  connection's keys, as [Sealing](/sealing/) states. The server reads
  no message, and requires no form of one: no channel response is
  malformed.
- **The frames are the two parties'.** The scope and the channel
  numbers of the relayed connection's frames are minted by the
  connector and the acceptor as the parties of that connection mint
  them. They are inside the sealed messages, and nothing between the
  connector and the acceptor reads them.
