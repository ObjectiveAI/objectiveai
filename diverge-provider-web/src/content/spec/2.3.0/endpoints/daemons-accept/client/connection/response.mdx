title: 'Response'
summary: 'The sequence of channel responses that answers the channel, what ends it, and what a finish with nothing before it means.'
order: 2
draft: false
---

```text
[type: u8][scope: u32][channel: u32][payload …]    zero or more, one client frame each
```

- **The sequence.** Zero or more channel responses, each one client
  frame of the daemon protocol with no tag in front, in the order the
  connector sent them on its
  [frames](/endpoints/daemons-connect/server/frames/) channel. The
  channel response finish is the connector hanging up: it follows
  when the connector finishes its frames channel, when the connect
  scope ends, or when the connector's connection ends. A channel
  response finish that no channel response precedes states that the
  id names no connection whose half may be opened, or that the
  connector hung up before sending a frame.
- **The frame.** Each channel response is exactly one client frame of
  the daemon protocol, laid out as [Frames](/frames/) lays out a
  client frame: the type byte, the scope as a `u32` big-endian, the
  channel as a `u32` big-endian, and the payload, running to the end.
  The type is `1`, a request opening a scope; `4`, a channel request
  on a scope; `5`, a channel response on a channel the acceptor
  opened; or `6`, the finish of such a channel. The type `0`, an auth
  frame, never passes: the credential of a daemon connection travels
  in the announcement's `mode`, and the server finishes the frames
  channel of a connector that sends one.
- **The numbers are the connector's.** The scope and the channel in
  a frame are minted by the connector as a client of the acceptor
  mints them, and mean nothing on this wire: nothing between the
  connector and the acceptor reads them.
