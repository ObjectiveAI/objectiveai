The client opens this channel to watch the served subtree as the
container holds it.

- **The request.** The payload is the tag byte `10` and nothing more.
  [Request](/endpoints/containers-serve/client/filetree/request/) states its form.
- **The response.** A snapshot, one channel response per change, and
  the channel response finish when the scope ends.
  [Response](/endpoints/containers-serve/client/filetree/response/) states the sequence.
- **The proxy watches.** The server opens one
  [filetree](/container-proxy-endpoints/filesystem-serve/client/filetree/) channel on the
  [filesystem::serve](/container-proxy-endpoints/filesystem-serve/) scope it holds on the
  proxy of the container, and sends every channel response of that
  channel as a channel response of this one, byte for byte, until the
  proxy finishes it.
- **The tree is the container's.** The snapshot is the subtree as the
  container holds it at that time, less what the proxy leaves out of
  every tree. The root of the tree is the directory the request
  named. Every `path` is a sequence of components from that root.
- **What changes.** A change is every change in the subtree: a
  mutation this serve's asks make, and whatever the program running
  in the container does, as the proxy observes it. A directory the
  proxy could not watch is in the snapshot with `changes` `0`, and no
  change under it is sent.
- **Every channel is whole.** Each filetree channel begins with its
  own snapshot. Two filetree channels on one scope are two streams,
  and each reports every change.
- **Ended with the scope.** The channel response finish of a filetree
  channel is sent when the scope ends, after the last change. The
  client ends a filetree channel by ending the scope, and not
  otherwise.
- **Not the run's filetree.** The [filetree](/endpoints/containers-agents-run/client/filetree/)
  channel of the container's run is unaffected by a serve and shows
  the subtree as part of the container's tree; this channel shows the
  subtree alone, from its own root.
