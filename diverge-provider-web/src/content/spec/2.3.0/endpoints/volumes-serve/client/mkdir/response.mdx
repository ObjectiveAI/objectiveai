```text
[0]             ok
[1][message …]  an error, UTF-8
[2]             ephemeral: the volume keeps nothing written into it
```

- **The sequence.** Exactly one channel response, ok, ephemeral, or an error, followed
  by the channel response finish.
- **The message.** The bytes after the error byte are UTF-8, not JSON.
- **Ephemeral.** The byte `2` states that the volume's `persist` is
  `false`: it keeps nothing written into it, the change is refused,
  and the volume is as it was.
- **A finish with nothing.** A channel response finish that no
  channel response precedes states that the server could not serve
  the ask. The client decides whether to ask again.
