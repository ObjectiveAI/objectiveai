```text
[0][kind: u8][size: u64][mode: u32][uid: u32][gid: u32][atime: 12 bytes][mtime: 12 bytes][ctime: 12 bytes]
                the entry, 57 bytes: kind 0 a file, 1 a directory; every integer big-endian; a time is [secs: u64][nanos: u32]
[1]             nothing at the path
[2][message …]  an error, UTF-8
```

- **The sequence.** Exactly one channel response, the kind, the size, the mode, the owner, the group and the three times, the absence of the entry, or an error, followed
  by the channel response finish.
- **The message.** The bytes after the error byte are UTF-8, not JSON.
- **A finish with nothing.** A channel response finish that no
  channel response precedes states that the server could not serve
  the ask. The client decides whether to ask again.
