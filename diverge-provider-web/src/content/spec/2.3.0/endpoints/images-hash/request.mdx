The payload of the request is the tag byte followed by the request in
the postcard wire format, as the postcard reading of
[Notation](/endpoints/#notation) provides:

```text
[21][digest_len: varint][digest: utf8 …][0][nonce: 32 bytes]
[21][digest_len: varint][digest: utf8 …][1][platform_len: varint][platform: utf8 …][nonce: 32 bytes]
```

- **`digest`.** The manifest digest of the image, UTF-8, of the form
  `<algorithm>:<hex>`, preceded by its byte length as a varint. It is
  the `digest` of an image in the client's
  [images::list](/endpoints/images-list/) listing.
- **`platform`.** One byte: `0`, no platform; or `1`, followed by the
  digest of one manifest that the image index the `digest` names
  lists, UTF-8, of the form `<algorithm>:<hex>`, preceded by its byte
  length as a varint. It has the meaning the `platform` of the
  [`prove` ask](/shared/possession/#the-ask) has.
- **`nonce`.** The nonce, exactly 32 bytes, verbatim, with no length
  before them.
- **Bytes after the request.** A server ignores every byte that
  follows the `nonce` field. A request whose payload extends past the
  request is not malformed.
- **Malformed.** A payload with no byte, a payload whose first byte
  is not `21`, and a payload whose bytes after the tag do not decode
  as the request — among them a payload that ends before the 32
  bytes of the nonce — are malformed. [Endpoints](/endpoints/) states
  how a server answers a request it cannot read.
