```text
[0][from, postcard …][body …]   per message
[1]                             once, alone: the server relays no message
[2]                             once, alone: a listen is open already
```

- **The sequence.** Zero or more responses of the byte `0`, each
  followed by one message, as messages are sent to the client's
  identity. The response finish follows the last of them, when the
  client cancels, reads no more of the scope, or its connection ends.
  Or: exactly one response of the byte `1` and nothing else, and the
  response finish. Or: exactly one response of the byte `2` and
  nothing else, and the response finish.
- **One ahead.** The server sends a response of the byte `0` only
  when the client has taken every response before it on the scope.
- **The message.** A payload whose first byte is `0` carries, after
  that byte, the identity of the connection that sent the message,
  `issuer` as a varint byte length followed by that many bytes of
  UTF-8, followed by `subject` as a varint byte length followed by
  that many bytes of UTF-8, as the postcard reading of
  [Notation](/endpoints/#notation) provides, followed by the body, running to the end of the
  payload. No length precedes the body, because the payload ends where
  the body ends. The body is the body the
  [messages::send](/endpoints/messages-send/request/) request carried,
  byte for byte. This revision prescribes nothing about its content.
- **`from`.** The identity of the sending connection, as its
  [authorization](/authorization/) established it: `issuer` is the
  `iss` claim of its token and `subject` the `sub` claim.
- **Unsupported.** A payload that is the byte `1` and nothing else
  states that the server holds out no
  [`messages`](/capabilities/#messages) object and relays no message.
  It is the only response of the scope; bytes after it are not read.
- **Already open.** A payload that is the byte `2` and nothing else
  states that a listen scope is open already: on the connection, as
  [One open at a time](/endpoints/#one-open-at-a-time) provides, or
  under the identity on another connection, as
  [messages::listen](/endpoints/messages-listen/) provides. It is the
  only response of the scope; bytes after it are not read.
- **No error.** No response of this endpoint states a failure.
- **A finish with nothing before it.** The request was not served, as
  [Endpoints](/endpoints/) provides, or the client cancelled before
  the server had sent anything. The server does not say which.
- **Malformed.** A payload with no byte, a payload whose first byte
  is none of `0` to `2`, and a payload whose bytes after `0` do not
  begin with one identity are malformed.
