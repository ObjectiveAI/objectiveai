---
title: 'What a provider supplies'
summary: "Deploying and reaching containers, managing volumes, vetting images, and judging a dialling caller's credential into an identity."
order: 2
---

A provider supplies four traits: a container deployer (three methods,
one per image source), the container itself (MCP in both directions,
the agentic loop, Postgres and command conduits, files, a filesystem
watch, stop), a volume manager (five verbs over one caller-scoped
namespace), and an image checker (where no is an answer). A fifth
judges a dialling caller's credential and produces the identity every
other trait receives. Two concrete pieces come with them: a registry
handle for pulling caller-served images, and the laboratories registry
that lets connect and transfer resolve a laboratory id across
connections.
