---
title: 'Scopes and channels'
summary: 'How one connection multiplexes many requests: scopes opened by the client, channels opened by either side, and finishes that only a responder may send.'
order: 5
---

A **scope** is one request and everything that happens inside it,
numbered by the client that opened it. A **channel** is one exchange
inside a scope — one ask and its answers — numbered by whichever side
opened it, and the two sides' numbers are independent spaces. Only the
responding side may finish a channel or a scope, which is why a duplex
conversation takes two channels; and a channel that finishes with
nothing sent before the finish is itself meaningful — it is how a
responder says an exchange could not be served at all.
