---
title: 'Filesystem trees'
summary: 'A live view of a filesystem: one snapshot, then one frame per change, for as long as the view is held.'
order: 4
---

Every filesystem view begins whole — a snapshot of the tree — and then
reports one change at a time: nodes inserted, changed, removed, in
paths given as components rather than joined strings. A view that
begins whole is what lets an observer arrive late; a watch that breaks
simply ends the stream, which says nothing about the filesystem it was
watching.
